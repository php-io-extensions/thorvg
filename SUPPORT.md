# Support

- **Questions and bugs:** open an issue on [php-io-extensions/thorvg](https://github.com/php-io-extensions/thorvg/issues). Include `php --ri thorvg`, your OS and CPU, PHP version and build (NTS or ZTS), and the smallest script that shows the problem.
- **How a ThorVG call behaves:** the comments in [`lib/thorvg/src/bindings/capi/thorvg_capi.h`](lib/thorvg/src/bindings/capi/thorvg_capi.h) and ThorVG's sources are the reference; this extension adds only what the README's translation section describes.
- **Security problems:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not open a public issue.
